Ways to engage

Project delivery, dedicated capacity or recruitment.

Project delivery

An agreed scope delivered by MV.tech, with milestones, technical reviews, acceptance checks and handover. Suitable for a new application, a warehouse migration, reporting development or a defined integration.

Dedicated engineers or team

Assigned specialists work within your tools and delivery process for the agreed capacity and duration. Suitable when your leads need additional skills or ongoing engineering capacity.

Permanent recruitment

Your organisation hires and employs the candidate. MV.tech supports the role brief, sourcing and assessment for a long-term technology position.

Delivery coverage

More than the initial build.

The agreed scope can include the stages required to get the system into use and keep it maintainable.

  • Discovery and architecture. Requirements, current-system review, source mapping and the technical design.
  • Development and integration. Application features, data pipelines, reporting models, AI integrations and automated workflows.
  • Testing and release. Functional checks, data validation, integration failure handling and deployment planning.
  • Handover and ongoing work. Operating instructions, implementation documentation and separately agreed maintenance or further development.

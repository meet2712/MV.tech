Capabilities within those services

The specific work we are asked for most often.

Cloud data platforms (GCP, AWS & Azure)

Storage, compute, orchestration and warehouse design on Google Cloud, AWS or Azure: BigQuery, Cloud Run, Cloud Logging and Secret Manager; S3, EC2 and Redshift; Azure Data Factory, Azure SQL, Blob Storage and Data Lake ingestion.

Data warehousing & modelling

Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift and PostgreSQL with raw, staging, curated and reporting layers, fact and dimension models at an agreed grain, conformed dimensions, naming standards, query tuning and documentation.

Warehouse migration & cutover

Moving legacy databases such as SQL Server, data lakes and manual spreadsheet reporting into a cloud warehouse, with database discovery, source-to-target mappings, dependency and impact analysis, and stage-by-stage reconciliation before anything is switched off.

Analytics, BI & semantic layers

Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio and Streamlit reporting over curated views and semantic models, with metric definitions, refresh checks and alerts. Existing SSAS and Analysis Services models reviewed before they are rebuilt.

Data quality, validation & reconciliation

Checks that compare pipeline stages and source systems on record counts, date coverage, nulls, duplicates, totals and metrics, with outlier detection, alerting and a documented override path for the figures people adjust by hand.

API, webhook & spreadsheet integrations

REST APIs, OAuth flows, webhooks, blockchain RPCs and scheduled pulls from SaaS, finance and operational systems, with retries and transformation logic. Google Apps Script jobs and Sheets add-ons where the process genuinely lives in a spreadsheet.

Web scraping & data extraction

Maintainable Python collection from dynamic pages and authenticated portals, respecting source terms and rate limits, plus LLM-assisted extraction from documents whose layout keeps changing.

Business app & portal development

Operational apps, admin panels and customer portals with role-based access, forms, approvals and API-backed workflows, plus Streamlit and Plotly data apps for internal analysis.

Custom chatbots & AI assistants

Support and internal Q&A assistants connected to databases and APIs, with role-aware behaviour, source grounding and a clear answer when the source material cannot answer.

MCP servers & custom AI connectors

Model Context Protocol servers in Python, custom connectors, plugins and reusable AI skills that expose approved business tools and data to AI applications, with OAuth, managed secrets and logging designed in.

Blockchain & on-chain analytics

Cross-chain transaction data consolidated into a warehouse: transactions, logs and events decoded with contract ABIs and Web3.py, token valuation with FX reference data, fee proration, categorisation and staking-reward reconciliation.

Support, security & optimisation

Performance passes, monitoring, least-privilege access, OAuth and managed secrets that support SOC 2 programmes, deployment hygiene and support plans for systems already in production.

Which service do I need?

A quick way to choose.

"Our reports are unreliable or manual."
Data engineering consulting, usually starting with one dependable reporting flow.
"We are moving reporting to Snowflake or BigQuery."
Data engineering consulting, starting with discovery, source-to-target mapping and an impact analysis of what depends on the old estate.
"Two systems disagree and we reconcile by hand."
Data engineering consulting for the checks, or workflow automation if the matching itself is the manual part.
"People copy data between tools every week."
Workflow automation, starting with a single repeated hand-off.
"We need an app, portal or internal tool."
Custom software development, scoped around one complete user journey.
"We want an assistant over our documents or tools."
AI automation, RAG & MCP development, starting with a bounded knowledge set.
"We need a senior engineer in the team for a while."
IT staff augmentation, with capacity and overlap hours agreed up front.
"We need to hire someone permanently."
Technology recruitment, led by an engineer who understands the role.

Before we begin

Common questions about hiring remote data, AI and cloud consultants

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

What is the difference between staff augmentation and project consulting?

With staff augmentation, consultants add capacity within your team and work under the agreed direction of your technical or delivery leads. A project engagement gives MV.tech ownership of a defined scope and its deliverables. Responsibilities should be explicit in either model.

Can we hire a dedicated remote team rather than one consultant?

We can discuss a multi-role team around your workstream. Team composition, allocated capacity, start dates and availability are confirmed during scoping; the website does not imply that every role is immediately available.

Can consultants overlap our timezone?

Yes. We can plan overlap with clients in any timezone. Share the working hours you need and any release requirements so collaboration windows can be agreed. This does not imply round-the-clock support.

Do you also recruit permanent employees?

Yes. MV.tech also offers technology recruitment for permanent roles within a client's organisation. Recruitment and consultant staffing are separate engagements with their own scope and commercial terms.

How much does data engineering staff augmentation cost?

We quote after a scoping call rather than publishing a fixed hourly rate. Commercial terms are agreed per engagement once the role, allocated capacity, duration and timezone overlap are defined. MV.tech is a remote-first team based in Ahmedabad, India, with a lean model that avoids large-consultancy overhead. You can include a budget range in your enquiry if you have one.

How do we hire a remote data engineer in India through MV.tech?

Share the role, required skills, workstream, expected duration and capacity, preferred start date and the timezone overlap you need. We review fit, availability and commercial terms before confirming a consultant, then onboard them with agreed access, initial tasks, acceptance criteria and documentation. MV.tech is remote-first and based in Ahmedabad, India, with working hours planned around your timezone.

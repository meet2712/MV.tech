Before we begin

Questions people ask before hiring a data engineer.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

How much does it cost to hire a data engineer from India?

We quote after a scoping call. The figure depends on the role, seniority, allocated capacity, duration and the support expected outside overlap hours, so a number before that conversation would be a guess. MV.tech's remote model keeps office and account-management overhead out of the price, and we ask you to compare proposals on the same scope, seniority and support expectations.

Can a remote team in India work in our timezone?

Yes. India Standard Time overlaps every major business region for part of the day, and we agree the window in your local time before the engineer starts. Typical windows are 9:00 am to 1:00 pm US Eastern, 7:00 to 10:00 am US Pacific, 9:00 am to 3:30 pm in the UK, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm in Central Europe, most of the day in the Gulf and 12:30 to 6:00 pm in eastern Australia. Overlap does not mean round-the-clock support; any out-of-hours coverage is written into the scope separately.

Can we hire a fractional or part-time data engineer?

Yes. A fractional arrangement of a few days a week suits a small team that needs senior judgement on pipelines, warehouse modelling or BI without a full-time hire. The capacity, the days and the overlap hours are agreed up front, and the engineer stays the same person for the duration rather than whoever is free that week.

Will we get a named engineer or a bench?

A named engineer. You meet the person before anything is signed, review their experience against your stack and work with them directly for the duration. If the engagement grows into a team, composition, start dates and availability are confirmed during scoping rather than implied by the website.

What is the difference between staff augmentation, a scoped project and recruitment?

With staff augmentation the engineer works inside your team under your leads' direction for an agreed capacity and duration. With a scoped project MV.tech owns a defined deliverable, its acceptance criteria and handover. With technology recruitment you hire a permanent employee into your own organisation and agree employment terms with them directly. We clarify which arrangement you need before discussing candidates or consultants.

How does a trial milestone reduce the risk of hiring remotely?

Because you judge delivered work rather than an interview. The first milestone is small, written down and checkable: a pipeline that reconciles to the source, a model whose figures match the ones people report, a dashboard with documented definitions. If the result or the working relationship is not right, the engagement ends at the review with the work and its documentation handed over.

What should we share before asking to hire a data engineer?

The workstream or backlog, the source systems and target warehouse or BI tools, the skills you need, the expected duration and capacity, your timezone and the hours that matter, and any constraints around security or access. A sanitised example of the data is enough for the first call; credentials are never needed for an enquiry.

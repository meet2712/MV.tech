Before we begin

Questions about hiring permanent data, AI and cloud engineers

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

How does permanent recruitment differ from staff augmentation?

Permanent recruitment helps you hire an employee into your own organisation. Staff augmentation supplies consultants to work within your team for an agreed engagement. MV.tech Solutions offers both, and we clarify which arrangement you need before discussing candidates or consultants.

Which technology roles can we discuss?

Our focus includes AI and automation, data engineering, software development, cloud platforms, BI and analytics. Share the specific role and experience level so we can assess whether the search fits our technical focus.

Can you help with remote technology hiring?

Yes. Share the permitted hiring locations, remote policy, required working hours and employment arrangement. These constraints shape candidate suitability and should be clear before the search begins.

What are your recruitment fees and hiring timelines?

Fees, search scope, expected timing and any follow-up terms are agreed for the specific role. Complexity, seniority, compensation, location and interview availability affect the search. We do not promise a hire or a fixed start date before assessing the requirement.

Should we hire a permanent data engineer or use an offshore data engineering team in India?

Permanent recruitment places an employee in your own organisation, and you agree employment terms directly with the person you select. MV.tech Solutions also provides India-based offshore data engineering teams, with working hours planned around your timezone. We clarify which arrangement you need before discussing candidates or consultants, and our guide comparing staff augmentation, project consulting and recruitment sets out the differences.

How do you assess a data engineer's technical fit before our team interviews?

An engineer joins the experience and problem-solving discussions, whether the role calls for SQL and warehouse modelling, pipeline engineering, BI and semantic models, or LLM and MCP work. Before the search begins we agree the depth of screening, any assessment and your team's final interview requirements. The hiring brief separates must-have capabilities from skills that can be developed in the role, and shortlist criteria are agreed before sourcing starts.

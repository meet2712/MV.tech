Before we begin

Common questions about hiring an offshore data engineering team

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

Is MV.tech Solutions a data consultancy company for an offshore team?

Yes. MV.tech Solutions is a data, software and AI consultancy with offices in New York, USA, and Ahmedabad, India. For offshore data work, we provide senior remote data engineering and analytics capacity for ETL, cloud warehouses, BI, data quality and reporting, with overlap hours agreed around the client's timezone.

How is this different from generic offshore staffing?

The engagement is engineering-led. We help shape the scope, define acceptance criteria, validate the data and document handover. You can use the team like embedded capacity, but the work is still managed around a concrete outcome rather than resumes alone.

Can the team overlap with US, UK, EU, Gulf or APAC hours?

Yes. MV.tech Solutions works from India Standard Time and agrees a collaboration window before the engagement begins. Typical overlap is possible with US Eastern mornings, UK and European business hours, Gulf hours and Asia-Pacific afternoons.

Can we start with one part-time data engineer?

Yes. A first engagement can be a scoped milestone, a part-time specialist or a small embedded team. The right shape depends on the backlog, access needed, review bandwidth and how quickly decisions can be made.

What should we share before asking for an offshore data team?

Share the reporting or data problem, source systems, target warehouse or BI tools, desired timeline, timezone and any constraints around security or access. A sanitised example is enough for an initial call; credentials are not needed for an enquiry.

How much does an offshore data engineering team in India cost?

Pricing is agreed per engagement and we quote after a scoping call. A first engagement can be a part-time specialist, one senior specialist or a small embedded team, with capacity, duration and overlap hours agreed before work begins. The first milestone is written down with acceptance criteria, so you can judge the result before deciding whether to extend the team.

Should we choose data engineering staff augmentation or a scoped consulting engagement?

MV.tech Solutions offers both shapes. Staff augmentation adds embedded capacity that works in your project tools with visible updates and review notes, while a scoped engagement is managed around one concrete outcome; in both cases the work is engineering-led, so we help shape the scope, define acceptance criteria, validate the data and document handover. You can start with one scoped milestone, validate the result, then decide whether to extend into embedded capacity.

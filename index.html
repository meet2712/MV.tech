How remote works with us

Based in India. Working in your hours.

India Standard Time sits between the Asia-Pacific and American working days, which lets a team in Ahmedabad share a real working window with almost any client. Before an engagement starts we agree when we overlap, how decisions get recorded and how you see progress between calls.

See overlap windows for every region

  1. Agreed overlap, not vague availability

    A recurring window in your timezone for standups, reviews and releases. US Eastern mornings, UK and European afternoons, Gulf and APAC business hours are all routine.

  2. Written progress you can forward

    Decisions, demos and open questions are documented in your tools, so your stakeholders stay informed without another meeting.

  3. The people you meet do the work

    No account managers or hand-offs to a junior bench. Scope, estimates and delivery come from the same senior engineers.

Common questions

What people ask before they book a call.

More detail is in the full FAQ and on how we work.

What does MV.tech do?

MV.tech is a remote-first consultancy that provides data engineering, custom software development, workflow automation, AI development (including RAG systems and MCP servers), IT staff augmentation and technology recruitment. We are based in Ahmedabad, India, and work with clients worldwide.

How does timezone overlap work with a team in India?

We agree a recurring collaboration window in your local time before the engagement begins, for example 9:00 am to 1:00 pm US Eastern, 9:00 am to 3:30 pm in the UK, or 12:30 pm to 6:00 pm in Sydney. Standups, reviews and releases happen inside that window; written updates and documented decisions cover the rest of the day.

Why is MV.tech less expensive than a large consultancy?

You pay for senior engineering time, not for offices, account management layers or idle bench capacity. Pricing is agreed per engagement against a written scope. We do not claim to be the cheapest option; we aim to be the best value for senior work with direct access to the people doing it.

How does an engagement start?

Book a 30-minute call or email a short brief. We clarify the goal, current systems and constraints, then propose a first milestone with deliverables, review points and the overlap hours. Work starts only after that scope is agreed in writing.

Can MV.tech work inside our existing team and tools?

Yes. Through IT staff augmentation our consultants join your team in your repositories, ticketing and communication tools, under the direction of your technical leads, with agreed capacity and responsibilities. We can also own a scoped project end to end.

Is MV.tech the same company as MV Tech Solutions or MVTech?

Yes. MV.tech is the official brand, and mvtech.solutions is the official website. MV Tech, MVTech, MV Tech Solutions and MV Tech Ahmedabad are common search variants of the same company.

## Next evidence to publish

Add client-approved case studies with the problem, constraints, implemented work and verified before/after results. Where the client cannot be named, obtain approval for a properly anonymized case study. Do not turn the prospective example scopes on service pages into claimed client results.

Link specific certifications to their issuer verification pages when available. The Toptal Top 3% reference belongs to the linked individual profile, not every consultant or the entire company.

## Measure the funnel

1. In Google Search Console, submit `https://mvtech.solutions/sitemap.xml` and inspect the new service pages after deployment.
2. Review impressions, query relevance, clicks and index coverage by landing page. Allow time for recrawling; technical eligibility does not guarantee indexing or ranking.
3. Review GA4 `contact_intent` by landing page, service and method. It measures a click to contact, not a completed lead.
4. Connect completed Calendly bookings or CRM enquiries before reporting confirmed leads or conversion rates.
5. Record qualified opportunities and won work against their source. Prefer improvements that bring relevant prospects over changes that only increase traffic.

Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and completed-booking integrations were not configured by this repository update.

## AI discovery

Important content and links are present in HTML, with canonical URLs and relevant structured data. Public crawler access is allowed. The optional `llms.txt` summaries should remain factual and consistent with public pages; they are not a ranking mechanism or a prerequisite for Google AI search.

# MV.tech Solutions website content

The company message covers software development, data engineering, BI, AI, integration, dedicated teams and technology recruitment. Service pages describe the deliverables, business applications, engagement scope and implementation approach. Business solutions are organised around finance/reporting, operations, products and blockchain data, rather than claiming unverified industry deployments.

The company, delivery, contact and FAQ pages explain the business and engagement models. The company name is MV.tech Solutions and it is written in full everywhere; "MV.tech" is only what the logo artwork reads. It is a registered assumed name (DBA) of BB & KV Inc., a New York corporation, and that legal entity is named only on the About, USA, privacy and FAQ pages. The site states two offices, New York first and then Ahmedabad, at city level. The engineering team works from Ahmedabad; the site does not claim US-based engineers or publish a New York street address.

The structure was informed by the service coverage and buyer information on [Inexture](https://www.inexture.com/), [Tridhya Tech](https://www.tridhyatech.com/) and [TatvaSoft](https://www.tatvasoft.com/). MV.tech copy and capability descriptions are original and grounded in the existing service scope.

Founder experience and individual credentials are attributed on the Expertise page to [Meet Vaghasia’s public Toptal profile](https://www.toptal.com/developers/resume/meet-vaghasia), checked on 4 November 2026. Examples describe individual engagements, including work through talent platforms. They do not add customer testimonials, ratings, new partner claims or numerical project results.

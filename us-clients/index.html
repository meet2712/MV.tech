Common questions

What should US clients know before booking?

Is MV.tech currently a US company?

MV.tech is currently based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. A US company is coming soon. We will publish its details once it is established. Current project discussions and remote delivery are with our India-based team.

Can US clients discuss a project before the US company launches?

Yes. Book a scoping call or email contact@mvtech.solutions to discuss the work with our India-based team today. If a US entity is a requirement for your procurement process, raise that before committing to an engagement so we can confirm what is currently possible.

Can we hire a remote data engineer instead of outsourcing a whole project?

Yes. MV.tech offers embedded data engineering capacity and IT staff augmentation as well as scoped projects. We agree the role, allocated capacity, responsibilities and US timezone overlap before work starts. See our data engineer hiring options.

How much does data or AI consulting cost for a US client?

There is no fixed public rate card. Pricing depends on the systems involved, deliverables, technical complexity, access requirements and delivery capacity. Share a short brief and any budget range; we price a written scope or an embedded role before you commit. Our remote data team cost guide explains what to compare in proposals.

What should we send before the first call?

Send the business outcome, your current platforms and source systems, the main problem, your timezone, target date and any procurement requirements. A short description or sanitised example is enough. Credentials and production data are not needed for an initial enquiry.

Before we begin

Questions brokers and agencies ask us.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

What can an insurance broker automate first?

Renewal reminders and tracking, commission statement reconciliation, client intake forms and certificate requests are common starting points, because they recur every month and follow clear rules.

Can you integrate with our agency management system?

It depends on what the system offers. Many provide an API, reporting exports or a database connection. We confirm the options during scoping and design around what is supported.

How do you protect client data?

Engineers use access you grant in your own environment, limited to what the work needs, and client data is not used to train third-party models. Tell us at scoping which privacy or financial regulations apply, and we state in writing what we can commit to.

Can AI read policy documents?

AI can extract fields from policy documents and answer questions about their wording with the source shown. A person should check the result before anything is sent to a client or relied on for coverage.

How does commission reconciliation work?

Each line on the carrier statement is matched to a policy and the commission you expected. Matched lines pass automatically, and differences in amount, rate or policy are listed for someone to follow up.

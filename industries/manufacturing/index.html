The starting point

Signs that plant data is harder to use than it should be.

  • Production, scrap and downtime figures are compiled by hand at the end of each shift or week.
  • The ERP holds the data, but few people trust the reports that come out of it.
  • Quotes, work orders or quality records move around by email and paper.

Business applications

Common first projects in manufacturing.

  • A daily production report. Replace the hand-built shift or weekly report with one that refreshes on its own.
  • ERP figures people trust. Reconcile one disputed report, such as inventory value or cost of goods, to the ledger.

Engagement scope

Start with one report or record people argue about.

Pick one report or record, such as scrap by line or the status of open work orders, and the person who owns it. Before we begin

Questions manufacturers ask us.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

What can data engineering do for a manufacturer?

It brings ERP, shop-floor and quality data into one place with agreed definitions, so output, scrap, downtime and cost can be reported daily without manual work and compared across lines, shifts and plants.

Can you connect our ERP and machine data?

Usually, yes. ERP data is read through its API, database or exports. Machine and quality data depends on what the equipment or its historian exposes, which we check during scoping.

Do you build predictive maintenance models?

We start with reliable collection and reporting of downtime and failures. Prediction is worth attempting once failure history is recorded consistently, and we will tell you if your data does not support it yet.

Can AI answer questions from our procedures and manuals?

Yes. A knowledge assistant can search approved documents and answer with the source shown, limited to what each person is allowed to see. It should say when the answer is not in the documents.

What does a first project look like?

One report or one workflow with a named owner, delivered in weeks, with written checks for accepting it. Further sources and reports are added after the first one is in daily use.

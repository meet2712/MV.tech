Before we begin

Questions e-commerce companies ask us.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

Why do the ad platforms and our store show different revenue?

They count differently. Ad platforms credit sales within their own attribution windows and can each claim the same order. The store records actual orders, often in a different time zone, and handles refunds, cancellations, tax and shipping differently. A warehouse model applies one definition to all of them.

How do we get one view of sales across our store, marketplaces and ad platforms?

Load orders, refunds, fees and ad spend from each source into one warehouse on a schedule, map products and channels to shared keys, and report from that model instead of from each platform’s dashboard.

Which metrics should an e-commerce dashboard show first?

Start with contribution margin by product and channel, customer acquisition cost, repeat purchase rate, refund rate and days of stock cover. Add more only when someone has a decision that depends on them.

Do you build online stores?

Our work is the engineering around the store: integrations, custom applications, data and automation. Where a custom front end is needed, we build it in React and Next.js against the platform’s APIs.

Can AI write product descriptions or answer customer questions?

Yes, as drafts. AI can prepare product content from your specifications and answer common questions from policies and order status. A person should review content before publishing and approve refunds or other actions.

Time-zone overlap

Working hours planned around yours.

India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) sits between Asia-Pacific mornings and American mornings, so a remote team in Ahmedabad can share a substantial working window with almost any client. We agree the collaboration window before the engagement starts and keep written updates flowing outside it.

Before we begin

Questions about hiring a remote data team in India

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

Can a team in India overlap US, UK, European or Australian hours?

Yes. India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) overlaps every major business region for part of the day. We typically plan 9:00 am to 1:00 pm US Eastern, 7:00 to 10:00 am US Pacific, 9:00 am to 3:30 pm in the UK, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm in Central Europe, most of the business day in the Gulf, and 12:30 to 6:00 pm in eastern Australia. Share your city and required hours and we agree the exact window for the engagement.

Does time zone overlap mean 24/7 support?

No. Overlap means agreed working hours for collaboration. Any support coverage, response expectations or release windows need to be defined in the engagement scope.

Why can a remote consulting team have lower overhead?

A focused remote model can avoid large office and account-management costs and keep clients closer to the specialists doing the work. The total project cost depends on scope, complexity and support; we agree the deliverables and commercial terms for each engagement.

How can we assess the team's experience?

Review our expertise page and the linked Toptal professional profile, then discuss experience relevant to your stack and workflow. The Top 3% credential belongs to the linked individual profile.

How much does a remote data team in India cost?

Pricing is agreed per engagement and we quote after a scoping call. The total depends on scope, complexity and the support you need us to own, and a focused remote team keeps office and management overhead low. Compare proposals on agreed scope, senior delivery time, validation, handover and support so the figure reflects the work you actually need.

What is the difference between staff augmentation and project consulting?

With a scoped project, MV.tech Solutions owns the agreed delivery scope and we agree the milestone, acceptance criteria and handover first. With an embedded specialist, which is the staff augmentation model, your leads direct the consultant workstream and we agree skills, capacity, duration and overlap. If you need a permanent role in your organization, recruitment is a separate model where you select and employ the candidate.

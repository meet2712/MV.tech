Before we begin

Questions about remote consulting team.

Can a team in India overlap US, UK, European or Australian hours?

Yes. MV.tech can plan collaboration with clients in any timezone. Share the city or timezone and the hours you require so we can agree practical overlap for the engagement. Collaboration windows and support coverage are agreed separately.

Does timezone overlap mean 24/7 support?

No. Overlap means agreed working hours for collaboration. Any support coverage, response expectations or release windows need to be defined in the engagement scope.

Why can a remote consulting team have lower overhead?

A focused remote model can avoid large office and account-management costs and keep clients closer to the specialists doing the work. The total project cost depends on scope, complexity and support; we agree the deliverables and commercial terms for each engagement.

How can we assess the team's experience?

Review our expertise page and the linked Toptal professional profile, then discuss experience relevant to your stack and workflow. The Top 3% credential belongs to the linked individual profile.

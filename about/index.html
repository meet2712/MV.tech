About MV.tech Solutions

An engineering company for software, data and AI.

MV.tech Solutions helps businesses develop applications, build data platforms and introduce AI into products and operations. Founded in 2022, we work from offices in New York, USA, and Ahmedabad, India, through project engagements, dedicated engineering capacity and technology recruitment.

Discuss your project Explore our services

The company

Engineering across the systems your business depends on.

A business application needs working integrations. A reporting platform needs consistent data. An AI feature needs access to the right information. MV.tech Solutions brings these disciplines together so the technical scope can follow the business requirement across software, data and analytics.

We work with business owners, product teams and internal engineers on new development and existing systems. Engagements can cover an entire agreed project or a particular workstream, with responsibilities, delivery reviews and operating ownership defined before work begins.

What we do

Build, connect and improve.

Software and business applications

Web applications, customer portals, internal tools and APIs that support the way your teams and users work.

Data platforms and analytics

Data integration, warehouse migration, modeling, business reporting and reconciliation across systems.

AI and automation

Knowledge assistants, document processing, MCP connectors and workflows that connect company information to business tasks.

Working with MV.tech Solutions

Choose the responsibility you want us to take.

A delivery relationship should be clear about who sets priorities, who does the work and how progress is reviewed.

  • Project delivery. MV.tech Solutions delivers the agreed requirements, with milestones and acceptance criteria.
  • Dedicated capacity. Our specialists work within your team’s tools and delivery process for an agreed capacity and duration.
  • Permanent recruitment. We support sourcing and technical assessment while your organization makes the hiring decision and employs the candidate.

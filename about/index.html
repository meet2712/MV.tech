About MV.tech

A Remote-First Team of Senior Engineering Specialists.

Our focused team brings together top-tier consultants across data, software, cloud, automation, and AI. Delivery includes Toptal-vetted Top 3% talent and GCP and IBM certified capability, without the account layers and operating overhead of a large consultancy.

Although MV.tech is a compact company, the delivery approach is shaped by work in larger enterprise and platform environments, including experience connected with Google, Volvo, BCW, RootstockLabs, Chainlabs, Toptal, and Turing.

How we operate

What "remote-first and lean" means in practice.

Senior people, end to end

The engineers who scope the work build it, review it and hand it over. There is no junior bench behind the proposal and no account manager between you and the team.

Your hours, agreed in advance

India Standard Time overlaps every major business region for part of the day. We fix a collaboration window in your local time and keep decisions written down between calls.

Overhead you do not pay for

No offices to fund, no sales layer, no idle capacity. Pricing is agreed per engagement against a written scope, so the cost reflects the work rather than the brand.

Scope before commitment

Every engagement starts with a first milestone, acceptance criteria and review points. You can judge the working relationship on something real before expanding it.

Careful with your data

Least-privilege access, separated environments and credentials handled the way you would expect from an engineering team that also runs production systems. See our delivery standards.

Honest about fit

If a problem does not need us, or needs something other than software, we say so on the first call. Long relationships come from useful advice, not from selling every project.

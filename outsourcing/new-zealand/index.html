Before we begin

Questions New Zealand companies ask before outsourcing.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

Is the time difference between New Zealand and India workable?

Yes, for work that can be written down and reviewed. We share about two and a half to three and a half hours late in your afternoon for calls and decisions. The rest runs on tickets, recorded walkthroughs and pull requests, and our team works while you are offline.

What kind of work suits this arrangement, and what does not?

Projects with clear requirements, backlog items, data pipelines and reporting suit it well. Work that needs constant real-time conversation, such as early product discovery or live incident response, needs more overlap than we can offer from India, and we will say so.

How do you handle New Zealand privacy requirements?

The Privacy Act 2020 restricts disclosing personal information overseas unless comparable safeguards are in place. Most organizations keep personal data in their own environment and give offshore engineers controlled access or masked data. We agree the terms with you before work starts.

Can you also cover our Australian team?

Yes. Sydney and Melbourne share a longer afternoon window with India, so a trans-Tasman team can use the Australian afternoon for joint sessions.

How do we start?

Send a short brief or book a call in the shared window. We reply with questions and a proposal that sets out scope, hours, people and price.

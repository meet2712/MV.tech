Before we begin

Questions Australian companies ask before outsourcing.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

How do the time zones work between Australia and India?

India’s morning is the Australian east coast’s afternoon. We typically share 12:30 pm to 6:00 pm in Sydney and Melbourne and 11:00 am to 6:00 pm in Perth. After you finish, our team continues for several hours, so handed-over work has moved by your next morning.

Does the Privacy Act allow our data to be handled offshore?

APP 8 generally keeps an Australian organization accountable when personal information is disclosed overseas, so most organizations keep personal data in their own Australian environment and give offshore engineers controlled access or masked data. We agree the access model with you before work starts.

Is offshore cheaper than hiring locally in Australia?

Usually for the engineering time itself, but compare the whole cost: your team’s review time, the overlap you need and the support included. We quote after a scoping call so the price reflects the actual work.

Do you work with New Zealand companies too?

Yes. New Zealand is two hours ahead of Sydney, so the shared window is shorter and written handovers matter more. Our New Zealand page explains how we plan it.

Can we meet the engineers before committing?

Yes. You speak with the engineer who would do the work, and you can ask them to walk through a similar project before you sign.

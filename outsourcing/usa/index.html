Before we begin

Questions US companies ask before outsourcing.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

Is MV.tech Solutions a US company?

Yes. MV.tech Solutions is the registered business name of a New York corporation headquartered in Bellerose, New York. Engineering is delivered by our team in Ahmedabad, India, and US clients contract with the New York company.

How many hours a day will the team overlap with us?

We typically plan 9:00 am to 1:00 pm Eastern or 7:00 to 10:00 am Pacific for meetings, reviews and pairing. The exact window is written into the engagement. Outside it, progress notes and pull requests are ready for your team the next morning.

Can you sign our MSA and NDA?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before detailed discussions and can work under your master services agreement, with a statement of work for each engagement.

How is our data protected when engineers are in India?

Engineers use access you grant in your own cloud accounts, limited to what the work needs, and secrets stay in your secret manager. We do not keep client data after handover unless a support agreement requires it. If state privacy laws or sector rules such as HIPAA apply, raise them at scoping and we will say plainly what we can and cannot commit to.

What does outsourcing cost compared with hiring in the US?

We quote after a scoping call, because the price depends on scope, seniority and the support you want us to own. A project is priced by milestone and a dedicated engineer by agreed monthly capacity. Compare that with the full cost of a US hire, including recruiting time, benefits and management.

Can we start small?

Yes. A first engagement can be one report, one integration or one part-time engineer. More scope is added after the first milestone is accepted.

Before we begin

Questions Singapore companies ask before outsourcing.

Not answered here? Ask us directly or read the full FAQ.

What hours will we share with the team?

Typically 11:00 am to 6:00 pm Singapore time, which is 8:30 am to 3:30 pm in India. Requests sent after that are picked up the same evening your time, because our team works for several more hours.

How do you handle the PDPA when work is done from India?

Giving engineers in India access to personal data is an overseas transfer under the PDPA, so the recipient must protect it to a comparable standard. Most organizations keep the data in their Singapore environment with controlled access or masked data. We agree contractual terms with you before work starts.

Can you support teams across Southeast Asia?

Yes. Kuala Lumpur and Manila share Singapore’s time zone. Jakarta, Bangkok and Ho Chi Minh City are one hour behind, which lengthens the shared window by their clocks.

Is offshoring to India suitable for a small Singapore company?

Yes, if the first piece of work is small and specific. A first engagement can be one report, one integration or a part-time engineer, with a written milestone you accept before anything expands.

How is pricing set?

We quote after a scoping call. Projects are priced by milestone and dedicated engineers by agreed monthly capacity. The proposal states the currency and payment terms.

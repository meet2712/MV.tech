Frequently asked questions

Company and services

What does MV.tech Solutions do?

MV.tech Solutions provides custom software development, data engineering, warehouse migration, BI, AI development, MCP integrations, automation and blockchain analytics. We also offer dedicated engineering capacity, staff augmentation and permanent technology recruitment.

Where is the company based?

MV.tech Solutions has two offices: New York, USA, and Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Our team works from both offices, and we work with clients in the USA and worldwide in collaboration hours agreed per engagement.

Do you work with companies in the USA?

Yes. We have an office and team in New York, and our team in Ahmedabad works in collaboration hours agreed in US Eastern or Pacific time. See our offices.

Is MV.tech the same company as MV.tech Solutions?

Yes. MV.tech Solutions is our company name, and the name we use everywhere. MV.tech is the short form that appears in our logo.

What is the legal entity behind MV.tech Solutions?

BB & KV Inc., a New York corporation. MV.tech Solutions is its registered business name (DBA). Agreements with clients in the USA name the supplier as BB & KV Inc., doing business as MV.tech Solutions. See our company information.

Who are your services for?

We work with business functions, product companies and internal engineering teams that need software, data, analytics or AI delivery. The fit depends on the requirements, technical environment and working arrangement.

Do you build new systems and work on existing ones?

Yes. Engagements can cover new development, migration, integration, performance improvement or a defined backlog within an existing application or platform.

Frequently asked questions

Engagement and working arrangements

What is the difference between project delivery and staff augmentation?

For a project, MV.tech Solutions is responsible for the agreed delivery scope and acceptance criteria. With staff augmentation, assigned specialists work within your process under your delivery direction for agreed capacity and duration.

Can we work with a dedicated engineering team?

Yes. We can discuss a team for an agreed workstream, including data engineering, software, BI or AI skills. The team structure, availability, capacity and responsibilities are confirmed before onboarding.

Can your team work in our time zone?

We agree recurring collaboration hours in your local time zone for meetings, reviews and release decisions. Share the overlap your team needs so we can confirm coverage, including daylight-saving changes.

Is permanent recruitment the same as hiring an MV.tech Solutions consultant?

No. With permanent recruitment, your organization selects and employs the candidate. An MV.tech Solutions consultant or dedicated team works through an agreed services engagement. Explore technology recruitment.

Frequently asked questions

Scope, pricing and ownership

How are projects priced?

Project estimates depend on the requirements, integrations, existing systems, data volumes and delivery scope. Dedicated capacity is priced by the agreed roles, capacity and duration. Recruitment fees are agreed for the search. We do not publish a fixed rate card.

How long does delivery take?

The timeline depends on scope, technical dependencies, access and client review. After understanding those inputs, we propose a delivery plan with milestones and identify any discovery work needed before a reliable estimate.

Who owns the code and deliverables?

Ownership, licensing, repository access and handover are agreed in the engagement terms. Tell us about any procurement or intellectual-property requirements before work starts.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Support, maintenance and enhancements can be included in an agreed ongoing arrangement. Coverage and response expectations are defined explicitly; meeting overlap does not imply 24/7 support.

Frequently asked questions

Data and getting started

Can we sign an NDA before discussing details?

We can discuss a mutual NDA before detailed technical or commercial information is shared. A high-level description is enough for the initial inquiry.

How is access to our systems handled?

We agree the access needed for the work, use scoped permissions and handle secrets through the project’s approved process. Production access and release responsibilities are defined with your technical team.

What should we send with an inquiry?

Share the business requirement, current systems, desired outcome, target dates and time zone. A budget range and any procurement constraints help us discuss an appropriate scope. Please leave credentials and sensitive production data out of the initial inquiry.

How do we start?

Book a 30-minute call or email contact@mvtech.solutions. We discuss the requirement, clarify the delivery options and agree the next step before an engagement begins.

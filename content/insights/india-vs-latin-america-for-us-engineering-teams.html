In brief

  • Latin America shares most of the US working day, so it suits work that needs conversation all day. India shares about four hours with US Eastern mornings and keeps working after the US team stops.
  • India generally offers lower rates and a much larger pool of engineers, especially for data, cloud and AI skills. Latin America generally costs more than India and less than the United States.
  • Decide by the kind of work, not by the region. Many companies keep platform, data and back-office engineering in India and add Latin America for squads that work alongside US product managers and customers all day.

Which is better for time zone overlap?

Latin America. Colombia and Peru are on or within an hour of US Eastern time, Mexico City is one to two hours behind it, and Argentina and Brazil are one to two hours ahead. A team there can attend every meeting in a normal working day.

India Standard Time is 9.5 hours ahead of US Eastern in summer and 10.5 in winter. An India team that works into its evening typically shares 9:00 am to 1:00 pm Eastern, or 7:00 to 10:00 am Pacific. That is enough for a stand-up, reviews and a working session, but not for a day of ad-hoc questions. Which costs less?

India, in most comparisons of the same role and seniority. Published rate surveys differ and change every year, so compare written quotes for the same role instead of relying on averages. Add the costs that do not appear in a rate:

  • Your own time. A team with less overlap needs better written requirements and more deliberate reviews.
  • Seniority. A lower rate for a less experienced engineer is not a saving if your leads spend the difference supervising.
  • Structure. Hiring across three Latin American countries can mean three sets of contracts, payroll providers or vendors.

Which is faster to hire in?

The route matters more than the region. A services company with engineers already on staff can start in days or weeks in either place. Hiring individuals directly, or through an employer of record, takes weeks to months because you run the search yourself. India’s larger talent pool helps most when you need several engineers with the same specialist skill at once.

What kind of work suits each region?

India is the stronger choice when

  • The work can be written down and reviewed: data pipelines, warehouse migrations, reporting, backlog features, test automation, integrations.
  • You want progress while your team is offline, such as overnight builds, data fixes ready before the morning report, or support cover.
  • You need to scale a team quickly or need skills that are scarce locally.
  • Budget decides how much engineering you can fund.

Latin America is the stronger choice when

  • Engineers need to sit in on customer calls, design sessions and product decisions throughout the US day.
  • The work is early product discovery, where requirements change hourly.
  • You need people on call for live incidents during US business hours.

Should a company with an India team add Latin America?

Start by finding where the delay actually comes from. If work stalls overnight because a decision was not written down, a second region will not fix it. Clearer handovers and a slightly longer overlap usually will, at no extra cost.

Adding Latin America makes sense when some squads truly need all-day contact with US product managers or customers. The common pattern is to place those squads in Latin America and keep platform, data and back-office engineering in India. Count the cost of the split before you commit: another vendor or legal entity, a second set of working practices and more coordination for your leads.

How do you build 24-hour coverage with India, Eastern Europe and Latin America?

Each region covers part of the clock, and the handovers do the rest.

This works for support, operations and incident response, where each task can be finished or handed over within a shift. It works poorly for feature development, because each handover costs context. If you do it, use one tracker, a written handover at the end of every shift and one named owner for each open issue.

Frequently asked questions

Is India or Latin America better for a US startup?

It depends on the work. Choose Latin America when engineers must talk with your product team and customers throughout the US day. Choose India when the work can be specified in writing, when budget limits how many engineers you can fund, or when you need data, cloud or AI skills that are hard to find.

How many hours can an India team overlap with US Eastern time?

About four hours is typical: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm Eastern, which is 6:30 to 10:30 pm in India during US daylight saving time. The exact window is agreed per engagement.

Is nearshore always more expensive than offshore?

Rates in Latin America are generally higher than in India for the same role, but the total depends on seniority, how much of your own team’s time each model needs and how many countries you hire in. Compare written quotes for the same role and deliverable.

Can we use both India and Latin America?

Yes. Many companies place customer-facing or product squads in Latin America and keep platform, data and back-office engineering in India. It adds coordination and usually a second vendor or entity, so do it for a specific need, not for coverage alone.

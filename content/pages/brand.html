Using the logo

Five rules that cover almost every case.

  1. Use the vector masters. They are the source of truth. The PNGs are for everyday work.
  2. Match the logo to the background. Dark surfaces take the silver logo, white and pale surfaces the navy one.
  3. When you cannot control the background, use a file ending -on-ink or -on-white. Each carries its own background and clear space.
  4. Keep one dot of clear space. Nothing may enter the area around the logo equal to the teal dot's diameter.
  5. MV never appears twice. The monogram is the letters M and V, and its dot is the full stop, so a lockup pairs the mark with tech only, never with the full wordmark. Never retype the wordmark in a font.

Do not stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logo, and do not place it on a busy or low-contrast area. The guidelines in the download show the misuses in full.

Signal Teal and System Cyan do not meet accessibility contrast requirements for text on white, so on light backgrounds they are used for shapes and borders rather than words. Full contrast data ships with the download.

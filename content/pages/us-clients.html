Common questions

Questions from clients in the USA.

Is MV.tech registered in the USA?

MV.tech is based in Ahmedabad, India. We are setting up a company in the United States and will publish its details here once established. Our India team is available for project discussions now.

Can we discuss a project now?

Yes. Book a scoping call or email contact@mvtech.solutions to discuss the work with our India-based team today. Tell us about any contracting requirements at the start so we can confirm what is currently possible.

Can we hire a remote data engineer instead of outsourcing a whole project?

Yes. MV.tech offers embedded data engineering capacity and IT staff augmentation as well as scoped projects. We agree the role, allocated capacity, responsibilities and USA working hours before work starts. See our data engineer hiring options.

How is the work priced?

There is no fixed public rate card. Pricing depends on the systems involved, deliverables, technical complexity, access requirements and delivery capacity. Share a short brief and any budget range; we price a written scope or an embedded role before you commit. Our remote data team cost guide explains what to compare in proposals.

What should we send before the first call?

Send the business outcome, your current platforms and source systems, the main problem, your timezone, target date and any procurement requirements. A short description or sanitised example is enough. Credentials and production data are not needed for an initial enquiry.

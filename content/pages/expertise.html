Engineering practice

The operating detail is part of the implementation.

A system needs to remain understandable after the first release.

  • Data definitions and validation. Document transformations and business calculations, and compare important outputs with their sources.
  • Access and deployment. Use scoped access, managed secrets and a reviewable deployment process appropriate to the project.
  • Documentation and handover. Explain how the system is set up, how it runs and who owns operational decisions.
  • Project-specific acceptance. Agree tests for the workflows, data or features being delivered, including relevant failure paths.

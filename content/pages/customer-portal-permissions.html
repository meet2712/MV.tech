How to use the matrix before launch

  1. List who signs in and which records belong to a person, company or team. Include visitors who have signed out.
  2. Give each row one action, such as viewing an order or downloading a document. Have the business owner record the expected result first.
  3. Try the action with a test account and fictional records. Enter the result, assign an owner to each mismatch and repeat after the change.

Each download includes 30 rows with formulas. Replace the fictional entries or start in a blank row. You can use the workbook offline.

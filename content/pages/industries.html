How a first project is set up.

  • One process and one owner. We start with a single report, workflow or portal feature and the person who signs it off.
  • Checks agreed before the build. For reporting, that usually means totals that match finance. For a workflow, the cases it must handle and who approves exceptions.
  • Your systems and your access rules. We work in your environments where possible, with least-privilege access. See our security and delivery standards.
  • A choice of engagement. A scoped project, a dedicated engineer or team, or recruitment for a permanent role.

Before you choose

Questions about industry experience.

More answers are in the full FAQ.

Which industries does MV.tech Solutions work with?

We work across industries. We publish pages for logistics, manufacturing, e-commerce and insurance because their systems and reporting problems are distinct, and the same software, data and AI engineering applies to other sectors.

Do you need experience in our industry to help us?

Your team knows the industry and we bring the engineering. What matters is that we learn your process before building: which system holds each fact, how your team defines its figures and who approves a result. That is why a first project covers one process with a named owner.

Can you work with the industry software we already use?

Usually, yes, if the software offers an API, a database connection or scheduled exports. We check what each system exposes during scoping and tell you if a connection would be fragile.

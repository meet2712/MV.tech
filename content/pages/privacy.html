Privacy Policy

What personal information mvtech.solutions collects, why we collect it, who receives it and the choices you have. This policy covers this website and the inquiries you send us.

Last updated:

Who we are

MV.tech Solutions (“we”, “us”) operates mvtech.solutions. {{legal.line}} We have offices in New York, USA, and Ahmedabad, India. Questions about this policy go to contact@mvtech.solutions.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you email us, send a project brief or book a call, we receive what you choose to share. That is usually your name, work email address, company, job title and phone number, and details about your project, systems, timeline and budget. Please do not send passwords, credentials or sensitive production data; we never need them for an inquiry.

Information collected automatically

When analytics is active (see Cookies and analytics), Google Analytics records how the website is used: pages viewed, the page that referred you, approximate location (city or region, derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and clicks on our email, phone and booking links. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, processes the IP address of every visitor in order to deliver and protect the website.

Information from scheduling

If you book a call, Calendly collects the details you enter, such as your name, email address and any answers to booking questions, and shares them with us.

How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry and keep in touch about it.
  • To schedule and hold calls, prepare proposals and deliver services you agree to.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To protect the website and our business against fraud and abuse.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

How we share information

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers that run parts of our business for us: Google (website analytics), Calendly (scheduling), our email provider and GitHub (website hosting). They may use it only to provide their services to us.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, who are bound by confidentiality.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or when needed to protect rights, property or safety.
  • A successor business, if our business is reorganized or sold.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. Google Analytics runs on this website with Google signals and ad personalization turned off.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) to tell visits apart. We do not use advertising cookies. Calendly sets its own cookies only when you load the booking calendar.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal or a Do Not Track signal, this website does not load Google Analytics, and we treat the signal as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing of your personal information. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.

Tracking by other companies

Apart from Google Analytics, and Calendly when you load the booking calendar, we do not allow other companies to collect personal information about your activity on this website over time or across other websites.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the state you live in, US privacy laws, including those of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and Oregon, give you the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete your personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling (we do none of these); and
  • use these rights without being treated differently.

We honor these requests from any US resident, whether or not a state law requires it.

How to make a request

Email contact@mvtech.solutions with “Privacy request” in the subject line and tell us what you would like us to do. We confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. We may ask you to confirm details that show the request is yours, such as the email address you used to contact us. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission.

Appeals

If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying with “Privacy appeal” in the subject line. We respond to appeals within 45 days. If you remain unsatisfied, you may contact the attorney general of your state.

How long we keep information

  • Inquiry emails and booking details: as long as needed to respond to you and, for clients, for the length of the relationship plus the period our legal, tax and accounting records require.
  • Analytics data: for the retention period set in Google Analytics, never longer than 14 months.

Security

We protect personal information with reasonable safeguards for its sensitivity, including limiting access to the people who need it. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Where information is processed

Our team works in the United States and India. Personal information you send us may be accessed by our team in India and processed by our service providers in the United States and other countries.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not sell the personal information of anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data we handle for clients

Data we work with while delivering services for a client is governed by that client’s agreement with us, not by this policy. Our approach is described in our security and delivery standards.

Links to other websites

This website links to websites we do not control, such as LinkedIn, Toptal and Calendly. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

We post any change on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use personal information you have already given us, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.

Before you choose

Questions about outsourcing to MV.tech Solutions.

More answers are in the full FAQ.

Which countries does MV.tech Solutions work with?

We work with companies worldwide. Our regional pages cover the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, the UAE and Gulf, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and Hong Kong, because the shared hours and data protection terms differ in each.

Where are your offices and engineers?

Our headquarters is in Bellerose, New York, and our engineering office is in Ahmedabad, India. Engineering is delivered from Ahmedabad for every region.

How do you decide the shared working hours?

We start from the typical window for your time zone and adjust it to your stand-up, review and release times. The agreed hours are written into the engagement, along with how updates are shared outside them.

Is outsourcing to India safe for our data?

It depends on how access is set up. We work in your cloud accounts where possible, with least-privilege access and masked data where the task allows, and we do not keep client data after handover unless a support agreement requires it. Where your privacy law sets conditions for overseas access, we agree the terms with you before work starts.

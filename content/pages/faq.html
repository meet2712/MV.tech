The company

Who MV.tech is.

What is MV.tech?

MV.tech is a remote-first technology consultancy based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We provide data engineering, custom software development, workflow automation, AI development (including RAG systems and MCP servers), IT staff augmentation and technology recruitment to clients worldwide. The company was founded by engineer Meet Vaghasia and is led by engineers rather than account managers.

Is MV.tech the same as MV Tech Solutions or MVTech?

Yes. MV.tech is the official brand and mvtech.solutions is the official website. People also search for it as MVTech, MV Tech, M V Tech, MV Tech Ahmedabad, MV Tech Solutions or MV Tech Data Solutions. They all refer to the same company.

Where is MV.tech based, and where are your clients?

We are based in Ahmedabad, India, and deliver fully remotely. Our clients are in India and worldwide, including North America, the United Kingdom, continental Europe, the Gulf and Asia-Pacific.

How big is the team?

Deliberately small. MV.tech is a focused team of senior specialists across data, software, cloud, automation and AI, led by founder Meet Vaghasia. We scale a specific engagement with additional specialists when the scope needs it, and say so when it does not.

Has MV.tech worked with large companies?

Our engineers have worked with organisations including Google, Volvo, BCW, RootstockLabs, Chainlabs, Toptal and Turing, directly or through vetted talent platforms. These names describe experience, not endorsements, current partnerships or sponsorships.

Timezones & cost

Working with a team in India.

Can a team in India overlap US, UK, European or Australian working hours?

Yes. India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) overlaps the working day of every major business region for part of the day. Typical collaboration windows we plan around are 9:00 am to 1:00 pm US Eastern, 7:00 am to 10:00 am US Pacific, 9:00 am to 3:30 pm in the UK, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm in Central Europe, most of the business day in the Gulf, and 12:30 pm to 6:00 pm in eastern Australia. Exact hours are agreed per engagement.

Does timezone overlap mean 24/7 support?

No. Overlap means agreed working hours for collaboration: standups, reviews, demos and releases. Any support coverage, response-time expectations or release windows are defined separately in the engagement scope.

How does MV.tech keep consulting costs lean?

We operate as a focused remote team, so you pay for senior delivery time rather than offices, account-management layers, sales teams or idle bench capacity. Pricing is agreed per engagement against a written scope. We do not claim to be the cheapest option; we aim to be the best value for senior work with direct access to the people doing it.

How are projects priced?

Each engagement is priced against its written scope: deliverables, integrations, data volumes, testing, documentation and support. Embedded consultants are priced on agreed capacity and duration; recruitment fees are agreed per role. You receive the price before committing, and comparisons with other proposals should use the same scope and support expectations.

How do you communicate between meetings?

In your tools where possible: Slack or Teams, Jira or Linear, GitHub or GitLab, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. Progress, decisions and open questions are written down so your stakeholders stay informed without another meeting.

Services & delivery

What we build and how.

What does MV.tech build?

Data pipelines and cloud data platforms, dashboards and reporting layers, web applications and client portals, internal tools and business apps, workflow automations and API integrations, web scraping systems, RAG knowledge assistants, MCP servers and custom AI features integrated into existing products.

Can you handle both the product interface and the data backend?

Yes. The work can cover responsive front-end screens, API-backed workflows, data ingestion, warehouse modelling, analytics and deployment handover. The scope defines which parts your team owns and which parts we own.

Do you support or improve existing systems?

Yes. We can review and improve existing scripts, pipelines, APIs, dashboards, websites and applications while preserving current business workflows. A bounded review is often the most useful first engagement.

How do projects usually start?

With a 30-minute scoping call or an emailed brief. We clarify the goal, systems and constraints, then propose a first milestone with deliverables, acceptance criteria, review points and overlap hours. Work starts once that scope is agreed in writing.

What is the difference between a project, staff augmentation and recruitment?

In a project, MV.tech owns a defined scope and its deliverables. In staff augmentation, our consultants work inside your team under your technical leads for an agreed capacity. In recruitment, we help you hire a permanent employee into your own organisation. Each has its own scope and commercial terms.

Security & trust

How we treat your data and systems.

How do you handle access to our systems and data?

With least-privilege access granted by you, separated development and production environments, and credentials stored in your secret management rather than shared in chat or documents. Access is reviewed during the engagement and removed at handover. Credentials are never needed for an initial enquiry.

Will you sign an NDA or work under our contract?

Yes. Mutual NDAs before detailed discussions are routine, and we can work under your master services agreement or provide a straightforward engagement agreement covering scope, ownership, confidentiality and payment terms.

Who owns the deliverables?

You do. Code, infrastructure definitions, documentation and data models are delivered into your repositories and accounts, with ownership stated in the engagement agreement.

Do you use AI tools when building for clients?

Where it helps and where your policy allows. AI-assisted development is reviewed by senior engineers like any other code, and client data is not used to train third-party models. Tell us your constraints and we work within them.

Social Media Automation

Paste a link to a video you own or have permission to use and choose your channels. AI reads the original caption when available and transcribes the speech to draft subtitles and post captions. The platform formats the clip for vertical video and fits your branding around faces and on-screen text. An editor can preview and change the result.

Clips that pass the checks can publish automatically to the selected channels, including Instagram, Facebook, Threads and YouTube. A clip flagged for review waits for an editor. Each platform’s receipt stays with the clip. If an upload times out, the editor can check whether it landed before anyone sends another copy.

Report Answers

If someone on your team looks up the same figure in Tableau or Power BI every week, we can design an AI assistant around that report. Report Answers shows how the Model Context Protocol (MCP) can give an AI assistant read-only access to reports the signed-in person can open. The example draws on our founder’s reporting work.

Take “What were sales by region last quarter?” The assistant can find the relevant report, calculate totals across all matching records and show when the data last refreshed. Someone checking a figure can inspect the underlying rows and the reporting rules behind it.

Data Warehouse Migration

Our founder traced the joins, filters and calculations behind legacy sales and inventory reports. He built reusable SQL views in Snowflake and checked which calculations Power BI still performed in the reports themselves.

To check the migration, he compared record counts, date ranges and reported totals with the source systems. When a figure differed, the field mappings showed where to look, from a missing source record to a filter in the report.

Blockchain Analytics

Our founder built a BigQuery ledger that brought transactions from several public blockchains together. It tracked assets received and sent, network fees and transfers between chains. Exchange rates let the finance team compare wallet flows in US dollars.

If the team needed to change how a transaction was classified, it could record a correction in the reporting layer. Reviewers could still trace the figures back to the original transaction.

Production Data Pipelines

Our founder ran change data capture pipelines that copied changes from operational databases into Amazon Redshift. He investigated interruptions in replication, including failures after the structure of a source table changed.

He also kept scheduled reports running, restored refreshes after outages and set up alerts for incomplete loads. He documented how to reload the affected date range and what the next person on call should check.

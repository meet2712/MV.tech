Social media automation

One publishing queue for short-form video.

An editor can bring in a clip they own or have permission to use, check the crop, edit suggested captions and preview the finished video. The publishing portal keeps the approved version with its captions and the destinations the editor chose.

If two channels accept the post and a third times out, the editor can see the receipt for each one. The queue flags the uncertain result for review before another copy goes out. That detail matters when the same piece of content is going to several channels.

Custom business intelligence

A BI platform people can ask questions of.

Our founder worked on a reporting platform with dashboards, scheduled data refreshes and an AI assistant. Someone can ask which report covers a question, when its data last refreshed or what a metric totaled over a period. The assistant can look up the report definition and use the reporting system to calculate the figure.

The assistant uses the same report permissions as the person asking. Someone with access to sales reporting can ask about those figures. A restricted finance report stays restricted. Users can open the underlying report to check an answer.

Email and marketing operations

From a contact list to email drafts a person reviews.

We built a workspace that reads campaign rows, checks the sender and recipient, and prepares a draft in the right mailbox. It keeps approved copy where the team has already written it and shows which rows need someone’s attention.

Running the same batch again reuses an existing draft. A missing contact or an incomplete row can be fixed on its own. The sender reviews the message and chooses when to send it.

Marketing analytics

Checking which leads turned into sales.

Two reporting systems can give different answers to “Which leads became customers?” Our founder compared lead-to-sale matches from an internal attribution process with the results from an external provider. The work involved tracing which records each method linked to the same sale and identifying where they disagreed.

For a team commissioning similar work, a useful start is one campaign and one closed sales period. Agree which event counts as a sale, check a sample of matches together and keep unmatched records visible when reviewing the totals.

Marketing campaign production

A social carousel, ready for the person publishing it.

For a seasonal campaign, we designed a set of portrait slides for a social post. We delivered each piece of artwork separately, along with a preview showing the full carousel in reading order.

The preview let the reviewer check how the story read from the opening slide to the last. The individual files made it possible to change a single slide or hand the approved set to the person preparing the post. For a separate email campaign, we prepared the subject line, inbox preview text and message copy together.

AI usage reporting

Did usage dip, or is a day of data missing?

Our founder worked on a dashboard for a team tracking its use of an AI tool. The dashboard shows the dates covered by the available usage records and flags periods where data is missing. A person comparing two weeks can see whether both weeks are complete before drawing a conclusion.

The team checked how the live dashboard handled days with data and days without it. Leaving a gap visible matters when an empty day could otherwise look like a drop in adoption.

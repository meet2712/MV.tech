About MV.tech Solutions

An engineering company for software, data and AI.

MV.tech Solutions helps businesses develop applications, build data platforms and introduce AI into products and operations. Founded in 2022, we work from offices in New York, USA, and Ahmedabad, India, through project engagements, dedicated engineering capacity and technology recruitment.

The company

Engineering across the systems your business depends on.

A business application needs working integrations. A reporting platform needs consistent data. An AI feature needs access to the right information. MV.tech Solutions brings these disciplines together so the technical scope can follow the business requirement across software, data and analytics.

We work with business owners, product teams and internal engineers on new development and existing systems. Engagements can cover an entire agreed project or a particular workstream, with responsibilities, delivery reviews and operating ownership defined before work begins.

Build, connect and improve.

AI and automation

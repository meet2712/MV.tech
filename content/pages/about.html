About MV.tech Solutions

A software, data and AI engineering company headquartered in New York.

Meet Vaghasia founded MV.tech Solutions in 2022. Our headquarters is in Bellerose, New York, USA, and our India office is in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Our team builds web applications, data platforms and AI tools for companies worldwide.

The company

Why one team for software, data and AI.

Most projects touch all three. A customer portal is only useful if the data behind it is right, a dashboard depends on the pipeline feeding it, and an AI assistant needs access to the systems your team already uses. We build all of it, so nothing falls into the gap between two vendors.

We work with founders, product teams and in-house engineers, on new builds and on systems that already exist. Before work starts, we agree in writing who owns what.

What we do

What we build.

Software and business applications

We build portals, internal tools and APIs. We use Social Media Automation to publish short videos across channels. Clips that pass the checks publish automatically. An editor can revise a flagged clip and see which channels accepted it.

Data platforms and analytics

Data integration, warehouse migration, modeling, business reporting and reconciliation across systems.

AI and automation

Knowledge assistants, document processing, MCP connectors and workflows that connect company information to business tasks.

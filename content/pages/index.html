Before we start

Frequently asked questions.

More detail is in the full FAQ and on how we work.

What does MV.tech do?

MV.tech is a remote-first consultancy that provides data engineering, custom software development, workflow automation, AI development (including RAG systems and MCP servers), IT staff augmentation and technology recruitment. We are based in Ahmedabad, India, and work with clients worldwide.

Can MV.tech provide an offshore data engineering team?

Yes. MV.tech provides offshore data engineering and analytics capacity from India for ETL, cloud warehouses, BI, reporting, workflow automation and AI systems. We agree the workstream, required skills, allocated capacity and overlap hours before starting. See our offshore data engineering service.

How does timezone overlap work with a team in India?

We agree a recurring collaboration window in your local time before the engagement begins, for example 9:00 am to 1:00 pm US Eastern, 9:00 am to 3:30 pm in the UK, or 12:30 pm to 6:00 pm in Sydney. Standups, reviews and releases happen inside that window; written updates and documented decisions cover the rest of the day.

How much does an MV.tech engagement cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, delivery capacity and support. Projects are priced against agreed deliverables; embedded consultants are priced on capacity and duration; recruitment fees are agreed per role. Share the outcome, current systems and any budget range so we can propose a useful first scope and price it before you commit.

How does an engagement start?

Book a 30-minute call or email a short brief. We clarify the goal, current systems and constraints, then propose a first milestone with deliverables, review points and the overlap hours. Work starts only after that scope is agreed in writing.

Can MV.tech work inside our existing team and tools?

Yes. Through IT staff augmentation our consultants join your team in your repositories, ticketing and communication tools, under the direction of your technical leads, with agreed capacity and responsibilities. We can also own a scoped project end to end.

How can I identify the official MV.tech company?

Our official brand is MV.tech and our website is mvtech.solutions. We are the consultancy founded by Meet Vaghasia in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. If you find similarly named companies while searching for MV Tech or MVTech, check the domain, founder and official LinkedIn profile.

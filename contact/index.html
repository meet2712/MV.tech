What happens next
From first call to a written next step.

      
    
        
  1. 01
    We talk for 30 minutes
    You describe the result you need, the current setup and your constraints. We ask the questions an engineer would ask and tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    You receive a written proposal
    A first milestone with deliverables, acceptance criteria, assumptions, the overlap hours in your timezone and the price for that scope. Usually within a few business days.

  3. 03

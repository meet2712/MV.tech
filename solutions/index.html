Planning the solution

Agree the workflow before choosing the implementation.

We assess the systems and operating requirements with the people who own the process.

  • Users and responsibilities. Who uses the system, who owns the data and who approves business actions.
  • Sources and integrations. The applications, documents, APIs and databases involved, including access and refresh requirements.
  • Delivery and acceptance. The release priorities, test cases and measures that show the system meets the agreed requirement.
